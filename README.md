# Identifying clusters of athlete profiles

This project will look to cluster athletes into groups based on their historical training data and health statistics, aiming to identify where different training plans may be more effective and when certain athletes may benefit from training together.

The initial dataset used is The Longitudinal Athlete Sports Performance Dataset available from Kaggle.
